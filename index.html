HOW TO PLAY

DESKTOP CONTROLS

  • A / ◀ : Move Left
  • D / ▶ : Move Right
  • W / Space : Jump / Double Jump
  • S / ▼ : Duck / Drop through platforms
  • Shift : High-Speed Slide
  • J / Z : Plasma Blade Slash
  • Esc / P : Pause

MOBILE CONTROLS

  • Left Thumb: Move Left, Right & Duck
  • Right Thumb: Jump, Slide, Slash
  • Wall Jump: Slide down walls & tap Jump
  • Double Jump: Tap Jump again mid-air
  • Slide: Tap Slide while running to duck obstacles

OBJECTIVES

Guide the Aether Runner through 100 levels across 10 dangerous sci-fi worlds. Eliminate cyber enemies with your plasma blade, collect glowing Aether Crystals, trigger checkpoints, and reach the exit portal!
